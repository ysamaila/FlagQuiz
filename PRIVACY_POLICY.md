## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how **Flag Quiz** ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by **Yacxhub**, handles information when you play our mobile trivia game.

We are firmly dedicated to preserving your privacy. Flag Quiz is designed from the ground up as a **100% offline, privacy-first educational and trivia application**. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, gameplay logs, or device identifiers.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal Data
**We do not collect any personal data.**  
Flag Quiz does not require an account, profile, login, registration, name, email address, phone number, location, or any other personally identifiable information.

### B. Trivia & Gameplay Data
All quiz sessions, questions, answer selections, scores, and round results are generated and computed **strictly on your local device in real-time**. This information is never transmitted to external servers, cloud databases, leaderboards, or third parties.

### C. Device & Technical Information
**We do not collect device identifiers or telemetry.**  
The App does not harvest your IP address, Android ID, Advertising Identifier (GAID), MAC address, or hardware profile.

---

## 3. Device Permissions & Network Access

* **Zero Internet Permissions:** Flag Quiz does not declare or request the `android.permission.INTERNET` permission in its application manifest.
* **100% Offline Gameplay:** The complete dataset of all 195 UN member and observer states is bundled directly inside the App (`assets/data/countries.json`). Vector flags are rendered offline via bundled SVG/asset representations. The App works seamlessly in Airplane Mode and never initiates any remote network requests or background syncing.
* **Sensitive Permissions:** The App does not request access to your device's camera, microphone, storage, contacts, media files, or GPS location.

---

## 4. Local Data Storage

* **Offline Trivia Dataset:** Country names, capital cities, ISO codes, and regional data are stored locally as read-only bundled assets.
* **Local Preferences & High Scores:** If the App saves game settings or high scores, this data is saved exclusively on your local device storage using standard platform preferences. This information never leaves your device and can be permanently removed by clearing app data or uninstalling the game.

---

## 5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

* **No Analytics SDKs:** We do not integrate Google Analytics, Firebase, Sentry, Mixpanel, or any telemetry tools.
* **No Advertisements:** Flag Quiz contains no third-party ads and has zero ad-tracking SDKs.
* **No Third-Party SDKs:** The App contains no social media logins, tracking pixels, or third-party web views.

---

## 6. Children’s Privacy

Because Flag Quiz is an educational geography and trivia game that collects absolutely no personal data from any user, it is safe and suitable for all audiences, including children under the age of 13. The App complies with the **Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)** and the **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**.

---

## 7. Data Security

Because Flag Quiz operates entirely offline and does not communicate over the internet or store personal data on remote servers, your information is naturally protected from unauthorized access, network snooping, and external security breaches.
